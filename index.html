How It Works

Simple steps. No silence in between.

The thing clients hate most is not knowing what's happening with their project. We fixed that.

01

Send Your Brief

Tell us what you have, what you need, and when you need it. Footage goes to a shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder — no complicated systems, just a folder and a clear brief.

02

We Align

Before we touch a single clip, we make sure we both agree on what done looks like. Style references, pacing, deliverables — all confirmed upfront so there are no surprises later.

03

We Edit

We work. You get updates, not silence. When the first cut is ready, you'll know — and you'll have two rounds of revisions included to get it exactly right.

04

You Publish

Export-ready files, organized and labeled, delivered back to your shared folder. Everything named, formatted, and ready to go live the moment you open it.

Every project includes 2 rounds of revisions. Additional rounds available if needed. We'd rather get it right than leave you with something close.
